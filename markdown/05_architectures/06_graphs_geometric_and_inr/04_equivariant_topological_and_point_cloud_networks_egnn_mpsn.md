# Equivariant, Topological, and Point Cloud Networks: EGNN, MPSN, and PointNet

## Executive Summary & ELI5

When processing physical objects in 3D Euclidean space (molecules, proteins, crystalline materials, LiDAR point clouds, robot point clouds), models must respect fundamental physical symmetries. If a molecule rotates by $90^\circ$ or shifts by 2 meters, its physical energy must remain invariant, while its atomic forces must rotate by the exact same $90^\circ$. A model that fails to respect this symmetry must redundantly learn rotated views from scratch through brute-force data augmentation.

This topic explores the convergence of symmetry groups, computational geometry, and algebraic topology:
- **EGNN (E(n) Equivariant Graph Neural Networks)**: Achieves exact equivariance under the Euclidean group $\text{E}(n)$ (rotations, translations, reflections) without requiring computationally expensive Spherical Harmonics or Clebsch-Gordan tensor products. By constructing vector fields directed along relative difference vectors $\Delta x_{ij} = x_i - x_j$ scaled by invariant distance functions, coordinates transform equivariantly by construction.
- **PointNet & PointNet++**: Directly processes un-ordered point sets $P = \{p_1, \dots, p_N\}$ by applying shared multi-layer perceptrons followed by permutation-symmetric global max-pooling, overcoming permutation ambiguity without meshing.
- **MPSN (Message Passing Simplicial Networks)**: Transcends standard 1-hop graphs by lifting networks to Simplicial Complexes. By propagating messages between 0-simplices (vertices), 1-simplices (edges), and 2-simplices (triangles/faces) along boundary and coboundary operators, MPSN shatters the 1-WL expressivity barrier.

## Mathematical Foundations & Governing Equations

### 1. E(n) Equivariance and Invariance Definitions
An operator $f: \mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{Y}$ is equivariant under transformation group $G$ if:
$$f(g \cdot x) = g \cdot f(x), \quad \forall g \in G, x \in \mathcal{X}$$
For Euclidean group $\text{E}(3)$ (isometries generated by rotation/reflection matrix $R \in \text{O}(3)$ and translation vector $t \in \mathbb{R}^3$):
$$x_i' = R x_i + t, \quad h_i' = h_i$$
- Scalar features $h_i$ are $\text{E}(3)$-**invariant**: $h_i' = h_i$.
- Spatial coordinates $x_i$ are $\text{E}(3)$-**equivariant**: $x_i' = R x_i + t$.

### 2. EGNN Layer Formulation
Satorras et al. showed that equivariance is preserved by restricting coordinate updates to radial directions:
1. **Invariant Distance Metric**:
   $$d_{ij}^2 = \|x_i - x_j\|^2, \quad \|(R x_i + t) - (R x_j + t)\|^2 = \|R (x_i - x_j)\|^2 = \|x_i - x_j\|^2$$
2. **Invariant Message**:
   $$m_{ij} = \phi_m\left(h_i, h_j, d_{ij}^2, a_{ij}\right)$$
3. **Equivariant Coordinate Update**:
   $$x_i^{(l+1)} = x_i^{(l)} + \sum_{j \neq i} (x_i^{(l)} - x_j^{(l)}) \phi_x(m_{ij})$$
   Under rotation $R$: $\sum_j (R x_i - R x_j) \phi_x = R \sum_j (x_i - x_j) \phi_x$. Translations cancel out via pairwise subtraction.
4. **Invariant Node Feature Update**:
   $$m_i = \sum_{j \neq i} m_{ij}, \quad h_i^{(l+1)} = \phi_h\left(h_i^{(l)}, m_i\right)$$

### 3. PointNet Universal Approximation of Set Functions
A function $f: 2^{\mathbb{R}^3} \to \mathbb{R}$ on unordered point clouds is permutation-invariant if $f(\pi(P)) = f(P)$. PointNet parameterizes this via:
$$f(P) \approx \gamma\left(\max_{p_i \in P} \psi(p_i)\right)$$
where $\psi: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^D$ and $\gamma: \mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}$ are standard continuous multi-layer perceptrons.

## Tensor Memory Layout & Implementation Details

### EGNN Tensor Operations
- Coordinates: $x \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}$
- Scalar features: $h \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times D}$
- Adjacency matrix: $A \in \{0, 1\}^{N \times N}$
- Pairwise relative vector buffer: $\Delta X_{ij} = x_i - x_j \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N \times 3}$
- Pairwise squared distance buffer: $D_{ij}^2 = \sum_k (\Delta X_{ijk})^2 \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N \times 1}$
- Message input tensor: $M_{\text{in}} = [h_i, h_j, D_{ij}^2] \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N \times (2D + 1)}$
- Coordinate displacement weight: $C_{ij} = \phi_x(M_{ij}) \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N \times 1}$
- Coordinate update reduction: $\Delta x_i = \sum_j \Delta X_{ij} \odot C_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}$

## Pathological Misuse & Failure Modes

1. **Absolute Coordinate Injection into Invariant Messages**:
   - *Anti-Pattern*: Passing raw absolute coordinates $x_i$ directly into message function $\phi_m(h_i, h_j, x_i)$.
   - *Consequence*: Completely destroys translational and rotational invariance; moving the coordinate frame by $(10, 10, 10)$ alters predictions arbitrarily.
   - *Remedy*: Pass only Euclidean norms $\|x_i - x_j\|^2$ or relative scalar invariants into $\phi_m$.

2. **Unconstrained Coordinate Multiplier Drift**:
   - *Anti-Pattern*: Leaving coordinate projection $\phi_x(m_{ij})$ unbounded without clamping or tanh activation.
   - *Consequence*: Coordinate updates explode exponentially across multiple layers, producing infinite atom dispersions and `NaN` values.
   - *Remedy*: Apply bounded activation or weight normalization on coordinate projection layers.

3. **Loss of Permutation Invariance in Point Clouds**:
   - *Anti-Pattern*: Sorting 3D points lexicographically by $(x, y, z)$ coordinates before feeding to a 1D CNN or RNN.
   - *Consequence*: Tiny perturbations or rotations radically reorder the sequence, causing severe discontinuity and inference instability.
   - *Remedy*: Use strictly permutation-symmetric pooling operators ($\max, \sum$).
